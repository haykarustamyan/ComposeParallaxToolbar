# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and code review, is
expected to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Be constructive. Explain what you would change and why; give others the benefit of the doubt.
- Be inclusive. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks of any kind are not tolerated.
- Stay on topic. Keep discussions about the library and its use.

Maintainers may edit, remove or lock content that breaks these rules, and may block repeat
offenders. To report a problem, email highapps2019@gmail.com; reports are handled privately.
